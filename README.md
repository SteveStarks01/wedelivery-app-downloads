# WeDelivery Android downloads

Official signed Merchant and Driver Android beta packages. Application source remains private.

Download from the Releases page or either official website. APKs connect to the hosted WeDelivery service. They are direct Android downloads, not Google Play listings.

No account credentials are distributed here. Merchants register on the website; drivers receive accounts from their delivery company.

These packages are for beta testing. Push notifications, external payments and physical-device delivery behavior are not yet qualified.
